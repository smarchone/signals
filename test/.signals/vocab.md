# Vocab

| Term | What it is | Why it must exist |
|---|---|---|
| **Team** | the 2–20 people who use the product together | the customer (ceo/business#audience-smb) |
| **Workspace** | a team's shared space, created at signup | what a team signs up to reach (pm/onboarding#signup-2-screens) |
| **Session** | a server-side record of one logged-in browser | login state with instant revocation (arch/auth#server-sessions) |
